---
name: proofread
description: Proofread supplied prose for spelling, grammar, clarity, and consistency while preserving the author's meaning and voice.
---

Read the supplied text and identify its intended audience. Correct clear spelling and grammar errors, clarify ambiguous sentences, and preserve the author's meaning. Do not introduce new claims. Return the edited text and briefly explain any changes that affect meaning. If the user's intent is unclear, flag the specific passage instead of inventing a replacement.
